# YouTube Cover Reference

Use for YouTube video/episode/series thumbnails, podcasts, courses, and Shorts. Do not use for Channel Banner, Art, Header, or 2560x1440 channel branding; route those to `banner-design-director`.

Default: 16:9, 1280x720 or higher. Use Single mode for one video; use Series mode only for repeated episodes, channel continuity, or a recurring universe.

## Choose The Viewing Promise

- Vlog/lifestyle: cinematic place-memory, editorial whitespace, scene-integrated type; avoid default arrows and shouting faces.
- Podcast/interview: two-person tension or credible studio frame, visible microphone, authority and one clear argument; reserve a stable series marker if supplied.
- Tech/AI/coding: one recognizable tool/interface/object plus human consequence, controlled screen light, geometric type; avoid tiny code and scattered logos.
- Travel: location/landmark is the hero, explorer scale or personal journey, clean destination-specific light; do not hide the place behind a generic face.

Lock one structure before variants: face-vs-object contrast, dialogue split, destination scale, or a single scene with integrated title. Use one CTR vector: surprise, conflict, transformation, authority, aspiration, risk, or curiosity. Familiar grammar may support the click, but the specific subject, expression, scene, and claim must carry novelty.

For supplied references or current-category research, extract layout, title scale, subject scale, color, and proof device; do not mimic protected IP. If research is unavailable, use the brief and supplied assets without claiming live evidence.

## Series

Lock recurring title zone, palette/grade, typography, logo position, subject framing, and episode naming before individual covers. Vary one episode-specific hook, scene, emotion, or object at a time so episodes remain recognizable without becoming duplicates.

## Check

At feed size, the topic and dominant emotion/object must read immediately. Keep title short and approved, faces/object edges clean, contrast intentional, and all critical elements away from crop/UI edges. Reject template arrows, random stickers, fake metadata, noisy effects, title-only variants, and any output whose topic cannot be understood in under a second.
