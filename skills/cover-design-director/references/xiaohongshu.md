# Xiaohongshu Cover Reference

Use for 小红书/XHS/RED feed, note, and image-first social covers. Image covers support `3:4`, `4:3`, and `1:1` with no default. If the user has not already specified one of them, ask one localized single-choice `AskUser` card containing exactly those three equal candidates and wait before planning or generation; direct-generation language never bypasses this gate. `全屏笔记` is a note placement, not video; never relabel or route it as `竖屏视频` or `视频笔记`. Use `9:16` only for an explicitly requested vertical video-note cover (`视频笔记竖版`) or equivalent video placement, and never include it in the image-ratio card. Do not infer a ratio or video from `全屏笔记`, `全屏`, `竖屏`, or `竖版` alone. The cover must feel platform-native and made with real editorial judgment, not a clean generic AI poster.

## Direction Certainty

With a strong reference, account system, or series template: make one faithful direction, optionally three micro-variants within it. With a loose style: offer close, optimized, and differentiated directions. With no style: use three distinct feed jobs, not cosmetic color swaps. Asset-only work uses the assets as truth but still selects the route.

Search only when the user asks for discovery/trends or the route is genuinely uncertain. Prefer inspectable cover images; select one dominant grammar, 3-5 reusable traits (layout, title treatment, image relationship, palette/material, finish), and one rejection such as generic template collage. If image search is unavailable, use the internal route and do not imply references were found.

## Layout And Type

Choose one dominant feed vector: useful proof, human emotion, visual oddity, status/taste, or event participation. The title must be readable first, the proof/person/object second, and supporting detail third. Preserve room for text before rendering. Use real photos, screenshots, objects, handwritten marks, paper, cutouts, or controlled graphic layers when relevant; fake UI, random stickers, excessive gradients, and decorative data reduce trust.

Type must fit the route: marker/handwriting for diary, strong sans for utility or creator hooks, structured display type for event/3D, serif or quiet sans for editorial. Use no more than two type voices. Keep Chinese copy short and exact; do not make unsupported tags, dates, ratings, product claims, or platform labels visible.

## Check

At waterfall-feed size, identify the title, route promise, and proof cue in one glance. Guide covers must look useful and credible; diary covers lived-in; creator/IP covers personal; event covers operational; editorial covers intentional rather than empty. Reject plastic skin, pasted text, stock-like cutouts, template grids, overclean AI surfaces, and a cover that could belong to any platform.
