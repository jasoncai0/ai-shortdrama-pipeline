# Overseas Shortdrama Cover Reference

Apply with `shortdrama.md` for ReelShort, DramaBox, GoodShort, and other non-Chinese markets. These rules override domestic-market title language, casting defaults, genre encoding, and type treatment; shared source-of-truth, ratio, and identity locks remain active.

Default is 3:4 unless 16:9 or 9:16/full-screen is explicitly requested. All visible text uses the locked market language. Preserve exact titles; never add Chinese, pinyin, translated labels, fake episode data, or genre microcopy. For title languages with diacritics, use a two-step text-safe workflow when necessary: generate reserved title space, then composite the exact title with a glyph-supporting font.

Cast to the supplied references, story world, and target market; do not default to Chinese models for English-market covers. Use concise genre signals: polished wealth/contract romance, moonlit werewolf/alpha tension, betrayal/revenge contrast, power/awakening scale, game/apocalypse system pressure, family/heiress status reversal, or restrained dark fantasy. Keep live-action, anime/manhwa, and CGI tracks intentional rather than blended by accident.

Favor one legible couple/hero conflict, a clear power relationship, controlled background depth, and premium title treatment that survives phone size. Reject unreadable metal-logo effects, wrong-language text, ungrounded casting, flat AI faces, generic stock romance poses, fake platform UI, and any title whose accents or punctuation are corrupted.
